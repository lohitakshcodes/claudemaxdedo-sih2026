## 3. C3 Plausibility Audit (Why Raw FAR was 4% and CSI 0.52)

* **Physical Reality**: In operational NWP (ECMWF IFS / GFS) evaluated against rain gauges at point/0.25° scale for Heavy Rain ($\ge 64.5\text{ mm}$), raw CSI is typically **0.12 to 0.25**, and False Alarm Ratio (FAR) is **45% to 75%** due to convective displacement and double-penalty errors.
* **Why Our Prior Run Showed FAR = 4% and CSI = 0.52**:
  * In `simulate_controlled_dataset()`, the raw forecast was modeled as `fcst = obs * normal(0.58, 0.08)` (under-prediction) without generating realistic false-alarm convective storms in dry areas.
  * Only **10 false alarms** were generated across all 19,440 points!
  * With $H = 260$ and $F_a = 10$, $\text{FAR} = 10 / (260 + 10) = 3.7\% \approx 4\%$.
  * This is an artifact of the synthetic generator's lack of spatial displacement errors.

This is a classical post-processing trade-off and is reported honestly.
